Aayushi Singh

I'm an M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at UW–Madison, where I work on energy-aware cache replacement with Prof. Joshua San Miguel and with Prof. Setareh Behroozi on energy-efficient AI hardware for edge devices. I grew up in Abu Dhabi and came to Wisconsin for my B.S. in Computer Engineering. I decided to continue my master's at UW–Madison to keep building on the work I had started and because I really enjoyed the people and support around me. I've also enjoyed teaching throughout my time here, first as an undergraduate teaching assistant for digital design and microprocessor systems, and I continue that role now as a graduate student.

Outside the lab, I'm usually up for a board game, a game of Overcooked with friends, or some badminton. I'm also a huge fan of BTS and recently got to see them live in Boston.

Always happy to talk hardware, embedded systems, efficient computing, random niche interests, or pretty much anything else.
